About Our Organization

An International House of Wedding Architecture & Event Production.

Headquartered in New York with production ateliers in Milan and Udaipur, WedNow operates as a full-scale creative agency and logistical command center. Unlike traditional solo planners, our organization assigns a specialized 6-person department to every celebration.

From architectural CAD floorplans and floral botany to hospitality concierge and contract law, our in-house divisions collaborate under one roof so nothing is ever outsourced to chance.
